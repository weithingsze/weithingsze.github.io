Research areas

Digital health interventions

Designing and evaluating mobile and AI-supported tools for health behavior change, from feasibility through randomized controlled trials.

Dietary behavior change

How people change what they eat — and why some don't. Current work examines non-response to AI-based dietary interventions in type 2 diabetes.

Clinical trial methodology

Trial design and evaluation methods suited to rapidly evolving digital and generative-AI interventions.
