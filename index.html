Assistant Professor · The University of Tokyo

Wei Thing Sze, PhD

My professional journey began in pharmacy, but over time my interests expanded into digital health, behavioral science, and artificial intelligence. Today, my work lies at the intersection of health, behavior change, artificial intelligence, digital health, and user experience. I am interested in developing and evaluating digital approaches to support sustainable health-related behavior change. Despite this transition, I remain a pharmacist at heart, driven by the same goal of empowering people to live healthier lives.

My work has taken me across Malaysia, the United Kingdom, and now Japan, which has deepened my appreciation for diverse lived experiences, cultures, and perspectives that inspire me both personally and professionally.

I enjoy working across disciplines and learning from different fields to approach health challenges from different angles. I'm always happy to connect — please feel free to reach out if you're interested in a chat or potential collaboration.

Research areas

Digital health & lifestyle interventions

Designing and evaluating mobile and AI-supported tools for diet, physical activity, and everyday health behaviour.

Behavior change

The theory underneath the tools: how people change what they do, and why some don't respond.

Artificial intelligence in health

Generative-AI features in clinical use — how they are specified, validated against clinical standards, and made safe.

Personalized health interventions

Matching what an intervention offers to the person receiving it, rather than delivering the same thing to everyone.

User experience research

Understanding how people actually use health tools, and letting that shape what gets built.

Evaluation & acceptance

Whether an intervention works, whether people will accept it, and what the evidence needs to look like.
